The numbers on your Schedule E move. One of them moved halfway through 2026.

The IRS standard mileage rate ran at 72.5¢ a mile through 30 June 2026, then 76¢ from 1 July, a split year that is easy to file wrong. When a figure moves, we will tell you what changed and which Schedule E line it touches.

When it’s time to sell

Selling a rental? Keep more of your profit.

When you sell, the IRS taxes back the depreciation on your building, even the years you never claimed it. Reconstruct the wrong basis and you can over-report your gain by thousands. This free checklist is the exact list of records to gather before your CPA meeting, so your basis is defensible and your gain is right, not overstated.

Four parts: original cost basis, an improvements ledger, depreciation history including the years you missed, and the adjusted-basis hand-off to your accountant. Cross-checked against the 2025 IRS editions. You gather; your CPA computes.
