Inside

Nine more pages from the body

All 42 expense ledger pages (p.33 and p.74 here) end in a 15-box strip that totals the page by Schedule E line, so a year of entries adds up instead of just piling up. The twelve monthly rent rolls close the same way, though totalled by property onto Schedule E line 3 rather than by line — 54 pages that add themselves up, in all. The mileage pages carry the 2026 split rate — 72.5 cents to 30 June under Notice 2026-10, 76 cents from 1 July under Announcement 2026-11 — and p.82 totals the two halves separately onto Schedule E line 6. A book that prints one rate for 2026 gives you nowhere to split it.
