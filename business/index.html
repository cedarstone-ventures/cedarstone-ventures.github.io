How bulk orders work

  1. Ask for samples. Email us and we'll send images of three sample interior pages so you can judge the book before ordering anything.
  2. Tell us the book and the quantity. For 10 or more copies we prepare a written quote showing the total price, shipping, applicable taxes, estimated delivery, payment terms and cancellation and returns terms.
  3. Approve the quote. We place your bulk order only after you accept the quote and payment terms in writing.

Write to info@cedarstonellc.com with your company name, the book and roughly how many copies you need.
